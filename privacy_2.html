Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Support

Effective date: August 29, 2026

107 in 7 (listed on the App Store as Part 107 in 7 Days) is a study app that runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, and no network connection of its own.

The short version

We don't collect anything. Nothing you do in this app is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go — we operate no server, and the app contains no code that transmits data.

What the app stores, and where

So you can pick up where you left off, the app saves your study progress in its own private storage on your device:

  • Which lessons you've finished and where you are in the 7-day plan
  • Your attempted/correct totals, per-concept mastery, and readiness score
  • Mock exam results
  • Questions you missed, and when each one is next due for review
  • Best scores and times on chart drills, sprints, scenarios, and the tools
  • Your daily study streak
  • The three setup answers you gave at the start: roughly when your exam is, whether you've studied before, whether you have aviation experience
  • Whether reminders are on, and what hour you chose

That is the entire list. The app never asks for your name, email address, date of birth, phone number, or payment details, and it has no field to type them into.

This data lives in the app's own storage on your device, protected by iOS. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include it — that's between you and Apple, under Apple's terms. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

No accounts, no servers, no tracking

  • No sign-up, no login, no account, no profile.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs are built into the app.
  • The app does not read your advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app requests no access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, or health data. It has no reason to.

Reminders

If you turn on study reminders, iOS schedules them locally on your device. They are not push notifications: there is no push server, no push token is ever created, and nothing about your progress leaves your phone to produce them. The reminder text is assembled on the device from the progress already stored there.

Reminders are off until you turn them on. You can change the hour or switch them off entirely in the app's Settings, or in iOS Settings, at any time.

Links to official sites

The certification-journey screen links to official pages — FAA.gov, IACRA, faa.psiexams.com, eCFR — and Settings has an email link for support. Tapping one hands a plain web or mail address to Safari or Mail. The app sends no information along with it and adds nothing to the address. Once you're on another site, that site's privacy policy applies, not this one, and we have no way to know you went there.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both complete:

  • Settings → Reset all progress, inside the app, erases everything listed above and leaves the app installed.
  • Deleting the app removes the app and its storage from your device.

Because none of it was ever copied anywhere, that's genuinely the end of it. There's no server-side copy for us to delete, and no request for you to send us.

Children

This app is built for adults preparing for the FAA remote pilot certificate — the FAA requires you to be at least 16 to hold one. It is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children, and there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request access to or deletion of beyond deleting the app from the device.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top. If a future version ever collects or transmits anything, we will say so here and in the app before it does. We won't make that kind of change quietly.
