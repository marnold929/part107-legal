Channel 47

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026

Channel 47 does not collect personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, telephone number or postal address.
  • No account or profile — there is nothing to sign in to.
  • No viewing history is transmitted to us or to anyone else by this application.
  • No analytics, measurement or attribution software is embedded in the application.
  • No advertising software is embedded, and no advertisements are served.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers, because nothing is gathered.

What is stored on your device

The application saves one value in the Roku device registry: the number of the channel you were last watching, so it can open there next time. It is two characters long, it stays on your device, and it is removed when you remove the application. Nothing else is written.

Video delivery

Programmes are not hosted by us. They are streamed directly from public archives, principally the Internet Archive (archive.org) and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (oceanexplorer.noaa.gov). When your device fetches a video file, those services receive the request in the ordinary course of delivering it, including your device's IP address, in the same way any website you visit does. We do not receive that information, and we have no access to their logs. Their own privacy practices are theirs, not ours.

Roku's own collection

Roku collects information about how its devices and the Roku platform are used, under its own privacy policy, and that happens whether or not this application is installed. We do not control it and we do not receive it. See Roku's privacy policy at docs.roku.com/published/userprivacypolicy.

Children

Channel 47 is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age. There is no mechanism in the application by which a user could provide personal information.

Changes

If the application ever begins collecting anything, this page will say so before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
