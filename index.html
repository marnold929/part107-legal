Common questions

Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Everything the app knows about your studying is stored on that one iPhone and never leaves it. That's deliberate — there are no accounts and no servers — but it does mean starting on a new phone starts you fresh.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the app. After that everything works offline, including the charts, the practice questions, and the mock exam.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset all progress, inside the app. It clears everything and leaves the app installed.

The practice questions never seem to repeat. Is that right?

Yes. Weather reports, forecasts, and the loading and performance problems are generated fresh each time, so the numbers are different on every run. The chart drills draw from real sectional excerpts across six regions.

Is this the actual FAA exam?

No. This app is independent study material. The questions are written from the regulations and FAA references — they are not FAA exam questions, and no study app has those. You take the real exam at a PSI testing center.

Why does the app teach airport traffic patterns and runway markings?

Because the exam asks about them. Operations is about a quarter of the test, and much of it is about predicting where manned aircraft will be so you can stay clear of them.

The exam rules changed — is the app current?

The course is built to the exam blueprint in effect since September 29, 2025, where regulations carry the most weight. The chart excerpts identify their sectional and edition in each caption.

How do I turn study reminders on or off?

Settings, inside the app. Reminders are off until you turn them on, they're scheduled locally by iOS, and nothing about your progress leaves the phone to produce them.
